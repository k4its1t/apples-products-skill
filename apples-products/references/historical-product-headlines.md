# Historical Apple product-headline corpus

Snapshot date: 2026-08-30. This corpus adds 42 official Apple records for retired products, retired generations, and one announced-but-never-shipped accessory. Most records come from Apple Newsroom launch titles, subheads, image captions, or section headings; these are authoritative historical marketing artifacts but are not always product-page hero lines.

Use the corpus to study mechanisms and to catch accidental echoes. Do not reproduce or closely paraphrase the quoted wording. A date describes the source, not a current product claim. Recheck any fact before using it commercially.

## What the historical layer adds

1. **2000–2006:** longer, specification-rich subheads; tangible comparisons; category and price superlatives.
2. **2007–2012:** compressed equations, category redefinition, and a feature translated into a memorable unit of life.
3. **2013–2017:** shorter thematic declarations, product-family contrasts, and design-led “future” language.
4. **2018–2022:** more restrained update framing and editorial continuity around the experience after a product ends.
5. The most durable lesson is not the period vocabulary. It is the discipline of finding one provable product truth and expressing it at human scale.
