# Official Apple product-page pattern sweep

Snapshot date: 2026-08-30. Scope: current physical-product and first-party accessory pages linked from the Apple U.S. [site map](https://www.apple.com/sitemap/). Commerce-only buy pages, comparison grids, operating-system pages, and subscription services are outside this current-product sweep. For retired products and earlier launch language, use [historical-product-headlines.md](historical-product-headlines.md).

The excerpts below are short calibration samples, not a reusable phrase bank. Never reproduce or closely paraphrase them in generated copy. Open the linked official page when a request depends on a newer product generation or exact current claim.

## Cross-family findings

Across the sweep, the most repeated high-level moves are:

1. feature-to-benefit translation rather than specification restatement;
2. two balanced fragments separated by a period;
3. familiar idioms altered by one product-specific word;
4. small-versus-mighty, quiet-versus-rich, or thin-versus-powerful contrasts;
5. action sequences for workflows and everyday products;
6. proof-led superlatives on pro and measurable feature pages;
7. sentence case, short words, and confident terminal punctuation;
8. a more playful register for entry products and accessories, with more precision for pro products.

For official product capitalization and naming, consult Apple’s [Apple Style Guide](https://help.apple.com/pdf/applestyleguide/en_US/apple-style-guide.pdf) when exact nomenclature matters.
